TRAVEL CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Business Trip Cancellation

Employees should cancel flights and hotels as soon as a business trip is no longer required.

2. Refundable Bookings

When practical, employees should select refundable or flexible bookings for trips with uncertain schedules.

3. Cancellation Fees

Reasonable cancellation fees for an approved business trip are reimbursable when the cancellation was caused by a business requirement or approved change.

4. Personal Cancellation

Cancellation fees caused by personal decisions are not reimbursable.

5. No-Show Charges

No-show charges may be reimbursed when the employee can demonstrate that a documented business emergency or company-directed change caused the missed reservation.

6. Travel Credits

Unused airline or hotel credits resulting from a business cancellation should be retained for future approved business travel whenever possible.

7. Documentation

Employees should provide cancellation confirmations and any applicable fee details when submitting a cancellation-related expense.
