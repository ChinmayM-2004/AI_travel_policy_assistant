TRAVEL APPROVAL POLICY

1. Prior Approval

Business travel should receive approval before flights, hotels, or other significant travel arrangements are booked.

2. Standard Approval

Employees should obtain approval from their direct manager for domestic business travel.

3. International Travel

International business travel requires direct manager approval and department head approval before booking.

4. Client Travel

Travel primarily related to a client engagement should also be confirmed by the responsible client account manager.

5. Exceptions

Urgent travel may be booked before normal approval when delaying the trip would materially affect business operations. The employee must obtain retrospective approval as soon as practical.

6. Policy Exceptions

Requests that exceed travel or expense limits require approval from the appropriate department head. Finance may also be required to approve financial exceptions.

7. Documentation

Approval evidence should be retained with the travel request or expense submission.

8. Non-Compliance

Expenses booked without required approval may be delayed, rejected, or escalated for review.
