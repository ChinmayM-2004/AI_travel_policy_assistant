COMPANY TRAVEL POLICY - INDIA

1. Purpose

This policy defines approved business travel limits and reimbursement rules for employees traveling within India.

2. Air Travel

Employees at Manager level and above may book economy or premium economy for domestic flights longer than 2.5 hours. All other employees should book economy class.

3. Hotel Limits

Employees may claim hotel expenses up to INR 6,000 per night in Tier 1 cities and INR 4,000 per night in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. Taxes may be reimbursed when shown on the hotel invoice.

4. Meals

Employees may claim actual meal expenses up to INR 1,000 per day during approved business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

5. Local Transportation

Actual taxi, metro, or public transportation expenses are reimbursable when supported by receipts or valid digital transaction records.

6. Advance Booking

Domestic flights should normally be booked at least 7 days before departure unless business urgency requires otherwise.

7. Exceptions

Any exception to these limits requires approval from the employee's department head before the expense is incurred.
