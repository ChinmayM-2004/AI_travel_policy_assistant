AIRPORT TRAVEL POLICY

1. Airport Transportation

Employees may use company-approved taxis, rideshare services, airport buses, trains, or personal vehicles for travel between an airport and an approved business location.

2. Parking

Airport parking is reimbursable for approved business trips. Employees should select reasonable-cost parking and retain the parking receipt.

3. Checked Baggage

One standard checked bag is reimbursable when baggage is required for the business trip. Additional baggage requires manager approval unless required by the business purpose.

4. Lounge Access

Airport lounge access is not normally reimbursable. Lounge access may be reimbursed when required because of a flight disruption or when specifically approved by a department head.

5. Ground Transportation Receipts

Receipts or valid digital transaction records should be retained for airport transportation and parking claims.

6. Personal Travel

Airport expenses related to personal travel, vacations, or personal trip extensions are not reimbursable.

7. Flight Disruptions

Reasonable expenses caused by an airline cancellation or major delay may be reimbursed when the disruption affects an approved business trip and the employee provides supporting documentation.
