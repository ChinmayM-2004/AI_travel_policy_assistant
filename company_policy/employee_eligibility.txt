EMPLOYEE TRAVEL ELIGIBILITY POLICY

1. Eligible Employees
Full-time employees and approved fixed-term employees may claim business travel expenses when traveling for an authorized company purpose.

2. Contractors
Contractors are eligible only when their contract explicitly permits travel reimbursement or when written approval has been provided by the responsible department.

3. Interns
Interns may travel for business only with prior manager approval. Reimbursement is limited to economy transportation and policy-approved accommodation.

4. Business Purpose
A trip must have a documented business purpose such as a client meeting, company event, training, site visit, or other approved business activity.

5. Personal Extensions
Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons only after notifying their manager. Any additional personal cost is the employee's responsibility.

6. Remote Employees
Remote employees may claim approved travel expenses when they are required to travel to a company office, client location, or approved business event.

7. Ineligible Expenses
Expenses for family members, personal entertainment, alcohol, personal shopping, and unapproved travel are not reimbursable.
