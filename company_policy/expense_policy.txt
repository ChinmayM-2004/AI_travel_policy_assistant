EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Submission Deadline
Employees should submit business travel expenses within 15 calendar days after returning from a trip.

2. Required Documentation
Expense claims should include the date, amount, currency, business purpose, and appropriate receipt or digital proof of payment.

3. Currency Conversion
Expenses incurred in a foreign currency should be converted using the exchange rate provided by the company's expense system. If the system does not provide a rate, the card statement exchange rate may be used.

4. Meals
Meal expenses are reimbursable only when connected to an approved business trip or business meeting. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

5. Missing Receipts
A missing receipt may be accepted when the employee provides a written explanation and obtains manager approval. Repeated missing receipts may be rejected by Finance.

6. Personal Expenses
Personal expenses must not be submitted for reimbursement. If a personal and business expense appear on the same transaction, only the business portion may be claimed.

7. Duplicate Claims
Employees must not submit the same expense more than once. Duplicate claims may be rejected and may be subject to additional review.

8. Finance Review
Finance may request additional documentation before approving or reimbursing an expense.
