COMPANY TRAVEL POLICY - UNITED STATES

1. Purpose
This policy defines approved business travel limits and reimbursement rules for employees traveling within the United States.

2. Air Travel
Employees should book economy class for domestic flights. Premium economy may be booked when the continuous flight time exceeds 5 hours. Business class requires prior approval from a Vice President.

3. Hotel Limits
Employees may claim hotel expenses up to USD 250 per night in major metropolitan areas and USD 180 per night in other locations. Taxes and mandatory hotel fees may be reimbursed.

4. Meals
Employees may claim actual meal expenses up to USD 75 per day during approved business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

5. Local Transportation
Reasonable taxi, rideshare, rental car, parking, toll, and public transportation expenses are reimbursable when supported by receipts.

6. Advance Booking
Domestic flights should normally be booked at least 10 days before departure unless business urgency requires otherwise.

7. Exceptions
Any exception to these limits requires written approval from the department head and Finance.
